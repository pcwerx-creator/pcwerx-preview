Business Computer Repair

Slow, freezing or infected office computers cost you time. We repair business desktops, laptops and servers, remove viruses and spyware, and get your staff working again: remotely within 1 hour or onsite the same day.

Laptop Repair Hamilton

Laptop repair including hard drive data recovery, saving all your files, power jack repair, overheating fixes and much more.

Data Transfer

Did you get a new computer, or did your old one crash? Let us transfer all of your existing data to your new machine. We can move all of your pictures, movies, emails and any other work or school files. It might seem like a chore for you, but for our technician it’s a breeze.

Hardware Replacement & Upgrades

Is your computer outdated or not working at all? We can upgrade your computer or replace broken parts to get it working again. Don’t spend thousands on a new computer; we can get your old one working like new again. Whether it’s a new hard drive or SSD, more memory or a new power supply, we can do it all.

Remote PC Support

Don’t want to disconnect all of your cables? Contact a PC Werx technician to set up an immediate appointment to fix your problem remotely using a simple internet application. It lets us connect to your PC as if we were sitting in front of it, with remote support within 1 hour. Remote support can’t fix hardware problems, and your PC needs a working internet connection.

Computer Repair Hamilton

Professional Computer Repair including expert hard drive data recovery, safely saving all your important files and much more, including slow performance, virus removal and hardware upgrades.

Data Recovery from Hard Drives

We can recover data from PC and laptop hard drives. Did you accidentally delete your pictures, songs or presentation you’ve been working on all week? Did your computer crash and you need to recover all the data from the old hard drive and move it to the new one? We can recover lost data from a variety of hard drives and copy it to a new drive or USB drive.

Data Backup

Data backup is probably the most important part of IT infrastructure. All electronic devices will fail at one point; it’s not a question of IF it will fail, but WHEN. Are you prepared for your computer crashing, and losing all your data? There are dozens of choices out there; let PC Werx pick the right one for your home.
